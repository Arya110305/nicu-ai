### Note:
The BACT/ALERT system is an automated continuous monitoring system for blood cultures that detects microbial growth by measuring color change caused by production. Microbial metabolism releases metabolic products, altering pH and changing the bottle sensor color from green to yellow. The system monitors this change in real time; bottles remain undisturbed during incubation and are removed after 7 days if no growth is detected.
